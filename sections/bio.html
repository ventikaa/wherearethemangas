Who Am I

MANGAS

MANGAS is the project of Aditya Rao — a punk / alternative rock act originally from Chennai, now entering a new creative chapter in Philadelphia.

The name is Tamil — மாங்காய் — raw mango. Unexpected. Still finding its flavor.

Blending hardcore energy, alternative rock, noisy textures, surreal storytelling, and emotionally driven songwriting, MANGAS explores identity, modern exhaustion, illusion, nostalgia, chaos, and reinvention. What began years ago as dreamy indie pop and experimental rock has since mutated into something louder, darker, more restless, and deeply personal.

Beyond the solo project, Aditya has spent years performing across India as a drummer, producer, and collaborator - appearing at VH1 Supersonic and Magnetic Fields while working across indie rock/pop, hip-hop, funk, and alternative music.
