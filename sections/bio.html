Who Am I

MANGAS

MANGAS is the solo project of Aditya Rao — an indie pop and experimental rock artist originally from Chennai, now entering a new creative chapter from Philadelphia.

The name is Tamil — மாங்காய் — raw mango. Unexpected. Still finding its flavor.

Blending dreamy indie textures, alternative rock energy, surreal storytelling, and emotionally driven songwriting, MANGAS explores love, identity, nostalgia, chaos, and reinvention. What began as dreamlike indie pop is now evolving into something darker, more expressive, and deeply personal.

Beyond the solo project, Aditya has spent years performing across India as a drummer, producer, and collaborator — appearing at VH1 Supersonic and Magnetic Fields while working across indie, hip-hop, jazz, and alternative music.
