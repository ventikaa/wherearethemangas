TURN THE WHEEL

‘Turn The Wheel’ is an alternative rock/punk track that confronts a collapsing world built on illusions, apathy, and silent obedience. It’s loud, urgent, and unfiltered - a break from the stillness of Rewind and a plunge into rebellion. Where Rewind stood in the ruins of Heart Orbit’s dreamscape, ‘Turn The Wheel’ lights a match to what remains. Lyrically, it questions the price of a future that’s already been sold - where truth is distorted, dreams are commercialized, and control is disguised as comfort. The chorus screams in defiance: “What’s the price of a future gone blind?” Raw guitars, explosive drums, and an aggressive vocal delivery drive the track forward - echoing the chaos of a mind refusing to stay silent.
